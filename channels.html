  • Strict: the last 30 completed days; at least 3 highs on the upper line and 3 lows on the lower line (within 10% of the channel's width); width no more than 8% of price; a sloped channel's closes must follow its line (R² at least 0.6).
  • Loose: the last 20 days, at least 2 touches on each line, width no more than 10%. Listed only for stocks that fail the strict test.
  • The channel: a best-fit line through the closes, with the upper and lower lines moved out to the window's highest high and lowest low. Flat means the line moves less than 0.10% of price a day.
  • Next upper / next lower: both lines extended to the next session, known before it opens. Close in channel runs from the lower line (left) to the upper line (right); gold means within 15% of a line.
  • Universe: NSE's top 750 by market value plus every F&O stock. Data: NSE's end-of-day bhavcopy, refreshed every weekday evening. Prices are as traded, so a split or bonus inside the window usually knocks that stock off the list until it rolls out.
